COMMON QUESTIONS

Clear boundaries.
No black box.

Will AI send messages without approval?

No. Your team approves customer messages, quotes, scheduling, and exceptions. We agree on approval and escalation rules before implementation.

Do we have to replace our tools?

We use your existing tools where practical. We review access and integration limits before agreeing on the build.

What does the pilot include?

One agreed channel and one operational workflow, with intake checks, routing, staff approval, failure testing, operating notes, and a measurement plan.

How will we know if it works?

We agree on a baseline before implementation, then review response time, completed handoffs, exceptions, and staff effort after launch. Results are not guaranteed.
