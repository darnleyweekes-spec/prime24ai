BEFORE YOU START

Service workflow questions

What does Prime24AI do?

Prime24AI maps and builds a service request workflow: capture an inquiry, identify missing details, prepare a response for staff approval, and track who owns the next step. Darnley Weekes is the founder and accountable implementation lead.

What does the $1,500 pilot cover?

One agreed request channel and one operational workflow, including intake checks, exception routing, staff approval, failure testing, operating notes, and a baseline and post-launch measurement plan. Additional channels or integrations require a separate scope.

Will it send messages or make decisions without approval?

Your team approves customer messages, quotes, scheduling, and exceptions. We agree on approval rules and a human fallback before implementation.

Do we need to replace our current tools?

Prime24AI uses your existing tools where practical. We review access, integration limits, and the current process before agreeing on the build.

How is Prime24AI Agent related to Prime24AI?

Prime24AI Agent is Prime24AI’s lead-response workflow offering. Its lead follow-up demo shows the path from a new lead to a reviewed next action. Prime24AI’s service pilot focuses on implementing an agreed operational workflow.

How will we know whether the workflow helps?

We agree on a baseline and measurement plan before the build, then review response time, completed handoffs, and exceptions after launch. Results depend on the process, tools, request volume, and staff use; there is no guaranteed revenue or time saving.
