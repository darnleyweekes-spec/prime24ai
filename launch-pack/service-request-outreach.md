# Prime24AI Service Request Pilot — outreach playbook

Updated 2026-09-29 for Darnley Weekes / Prime24AI.

## Offer used in outreach

Prime24AI is a done-for-you workflow implementation service, not a generic AI consultancy or another software subscription.

Primary offer: **$1,500 Service Request Workflow Pilot**

The pilot covers one request channel and one operational workflow: capture the request, flag missing information or urgency, prepare the next response for staff approval, record who owns the next step, test failures and exceptions, and document the handoff.

Primary link: [Prime24AI Service Request Workflow Pilot](https://prime24ai.com/#service-pilot)

## Qualification gate

Only contact a business when all of these are true:

1. The business has a working website and a published, verifiable business email.
2. A visible request, quote, booking, intake, or support process can be cited accurately.
3. The proposed workflow is realistic for one fixed-scope pilot.
4. The business does not already advertise a mature solution that removes the identified problem.
5. Sent mail has been checked so the business has not already been contacted.

Never claim to know response times, conversion losses, staffing burden, or internal software unless the business has published that information.

## First-email structure

- Subject: two to five words tied to the workflow.
- First sentence: one verified observation from the business's website.
- Second sentence: explain the manual handoff that may follow; use “may” or “appears” when it is an inference.
- Offer: describe only the relevant workflow, not every Prime24AI capability.
- Control: state that staff approves customer-facing messages or decisions.
- CTA: ask permission to send a one-page workflow map.
- Link: use the single service-pilot hyperlink above.
- Length: 80–120 words before the signature.

## Quality checklist before drafting

- The observation is visible and source-verifiable.
- The email names one workflow, not a broad transformation.
- No unsupported result, savings, or urgency claim appears.
- The recipient and published email are verified.
- Gmail sent history is checked for duplicates.
- The service-pilot hyperlink works and uses the correct UTM campaign.
- The approved graphic is embedded below the copy with alt text.
- The signature names Darnley Weekes and Prime24AI.
- Any promotional-email footer and opt-out requirements are included where applicable.
