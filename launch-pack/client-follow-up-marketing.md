3. Prepare a reminder: only the open items appear.
4. Mark the card statement Received and regenerate.
5. Show the one remaining correction request.
6. Show CSV export and backup; state that there is no cloud sync.
7. Close: “The sample is free. I can tailor this to one of your monthly workflows for $149.”

## Launch sequence and spending
Day 1: Confirm the live page and checkout. Publish the launch post yourself. Send three individual emails after checking past contact history. Contact the other two through their published route. Do not submit pitches to client-only support/upload portals.
Day 2: Run short demonstrations for respondents. Ask what they already use and how they track missing items. Record exact objections.
Day 4 or 5: Send the one follow-up above to non-responders who have not opted out.
After 15 qualified prospects: if no useful replies, improve the problem or audience before spending money. Do not infer price is the only issue.
After the first delivery: ask whether it helped, what changed, and whether you may quote their feedback. Never invent results.
