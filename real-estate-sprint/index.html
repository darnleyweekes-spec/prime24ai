The boundaries

What to know before checkout.

When does the 48-hour clock start?

After payment is verified, intake and required assets are complete, and Prime24AI confirms a start time in writing. You receive a page ready to review within 48 hours of that start. Launch depends on your approval and brokerage review.

What does the revision cover?

One consolidated list of edits to copy, imagery, colors, or spacing, requested within five calendar days after delivery. We return that revision within two business days. It does not include a second page or a full redesign.

Are a domain and hosting included?

We guide connection of one client-owned domain. Registration and any third-party hosting fees are separate. Confirm the hosting provider, cost, and account owner with us before payment. You receive the site files and handoff notes.

What if my brokerage must approve the site?

Send its required disclosures and reviewer contact in your intake. The review site can be delivered in 48 hours; public launch waits for the necessary approval.

What if the promised review delivery is late?

If Prime24AI misses the agreed review delivery time for reasons within our control, you may cancel and receive a full refund. We will confirm project terms and timing by email.
