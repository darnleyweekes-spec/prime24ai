# Client Follow-Up Kit — five editable reminders
Free starter templates from Prime24AI. Replace every bracketed field before sending. Use your firm's approved secure upload portal, not email attachments. Ask only for items your firm actually needs. Confirm receipt separately from approval.

## 4. Correction request
Subject: A replacement needed for [document]

## 5. Receipt confirmation
Subject: Document checklist complete

Hi [First name],

Thank you. All items on the [period] checklist are marked received. We will review them and let you know if anything else is needed.

This confirms receipt; it does not confirm completion of the bookkeeping.

[Sender]

## Repeatable routine
1. Duplicate last month's blank checklist; check the current period and agreed dates.
2. Remove requests that do not apply. Use aliases in the browser tool.
3. Send the first request through your normal account after reviewing it.
4. Review the portal before each reminder. Mark arrived items Received; record specific corrections in the item label.
5. Prepare and review the next draft. Record when you actually sent it in your firm's existing work log.
6. When all items arrive, send receipt confirmation. Bookkeeping review and approval remain separate.
7. Download the updated CSV and JSON backup to your firm's approved folder. This tool has no cloud sync.
